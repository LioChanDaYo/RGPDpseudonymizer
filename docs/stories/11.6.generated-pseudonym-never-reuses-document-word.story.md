### Context

**The leak.** The library draws a new name's pseudonym without looking at the document. Each draw is a `secrets.choice` in one of four loops of `pseudonym/library_manager.py`:
- first names `:584` (loop `:582-610`);
- last names `:637` (loop `:635-657`);
- places `:681` (loop `:679-690`);
- organisations `:713` (loop `:711-722`).

So a name can get one of its own words back. « Élodie Martin » can be written « Élodie Ramos » or « Sylvie Martin », and « Marseille » can stay « Marseille ». The real word is then in clear in the output.

**Measured** (Dev Notes "Measurements"). Real `neutral` library, real compositional engine, 2,000 runs per document, on the five realistic French documents of Story 11.5:
- **Own word.** 0.75% to 4.85% of runs leave at least one name with one of its own words: about 1 run in 133 to 1 in 21, depending on the document.
- **Another real name of the same document.** 0.50% to 16.35% of runs draw a part that is another real name of the document. This is Lionel's case: « Martin » written « Dupont » while the real Duponts became « Wagner ».
- **Either.** 1.25% to 20.60% of runs.
- **End to end, forced draw.** 11.5's probe wrote « Élodie Martin a présenté le budget à Marseille. » unchanged on `main`, 2.3.0 and 2.2.0. This SM's probe shows the same on every write path on `8bf265d` (« … de Datavora à Marseille. » unchanged).

**The epic's numbers.** The approved epic first said "about 1 run in 70 to 1 in 15", from 11.5's per-name counts, where a lone mention counts again. Counted per run (a run is a document finalized once), the measurement gives 1 in 133 to 1 in 21. The orchestrator corrected the epic's rate line to these per-run figures on 2026-11-10, in the working tree (PO re-read, v1.1). This story and its CHANGELOG use the per-run numbers. The epic's other figure, "1% to 22% of documents" for another real name of the document, is 11.5's; this story's per-run measurement is 0.50% to 16.35%. Aligning it is the orchestrator's call, before commit 1 (AC11).

**Custom pseudonyms are already checked** against the document (`core/custom_pseudonym.py:559-684`: Story 10.11's Q-C and Q-I, and Story 10.16's names of the review). The drawn ones are not. This story applies the document half of that same check to every draw. For a drawn place or organisation it compares every word, not only the whole value (Lionel's "widen"); the custom check itself does not change (Dev Notes "The rule").

### The rule

**A value the library draws is drawn again when one of its runs occurs:**
- **in the document**, as consecutive whole words with the run's own separators, the first of them starting with a capital letter there (found by detection or not; a word at the start of a sentence counts); or
- **in one of the names sent**, whatever their capitals: the mapping key of every entity `_resolve_pseudonyms` receives (after corrections, titles taken off).

Comparison is folded: capitals, accents and repeated spaces are ignored (`fold`, `core/custom_pseudonym.py:219-224`).

**Which runs, for a drawn value of any kind** (the person branch of `_document_runs`, `:438-446`, which Fix design 1 names `_runs_by_word`): every run of its words, except a linking word followed by another word, which is not compared alone (Q-I).
- A person's part: « Le Gall » is compared as « Le Gall » and « Gall », never « Le ».
- **A place or an organisation, widened (Lionel, "widen"):** the same, so every word counts. « Groupe Commercial Lyon » is compared as the whole value, « Groupe Commercial », « Commercial Lyon », « Groupe », « Commercial » and « Lyon »; in a document that names Lyon, it is drawn again. « La Place de la République » is never compared on « La », « de » or « la » alone.
- Words are cut at spaces, as for a person (`split()`). **Then each part of a hyphenated word is compared on its own too** (the orchestrator's reading of "widen", O15), for a person's part as for a place or organisation: « Saint-Denis » is also compared as « Saint » and « Denis », « Aix-en-Provence » as « Aix » and « Provence » (« en » is not a linking word of the Q-I list, so it is compared too, harmlessly), « Île-de-France » as « Île » and « France » (never « de » alone). So « Saint-Denis » is drawn again in a document naming « Denis Laval », and a drawn first name « Jean-Pierre » for « Pierre Martin ». A word joined by an apostrophe stays whole (« l'Ouest »): the ruling cuts at hyphens only.
- « Saint » and « Sainte » count like any other word. Measured harmless: on the five documents and a sixth naming « Saint-Denis », « Sainte-Marie Lefort » and « Saint-Étienne », counting them changed no result (`libshare116.log`).
- The custom check keeps a hyphenated word whole (« Jean-Paul » is not compared on « Paul »), as today.
- A word that is not a linking word is compared alone even when it is small: « et » in « Manufacture Nantes et Fils » is drawn again against a sentence that starts « Et », a harmless redraw (`stress116.log`).

1. **The rule, `core/custom_pseudonym.py`.**

- **The forced draw.** One helper, `_forced(front, last_names=None, places=None)`, a context manager, as the SM's `leak116.py` does it:
  - `front` names each word's own list: `{"first": [...], "last": [...], "place": [...], "org": [...]}`. A word heads **only its own list**: a `"first"` word every gender's first names, a `"last"` word the last names, a `"place"` word the places' first category (`cities`), an `"org"` word the organisations' first category (`companies`). (PO, v1.1: a word put at the head of every list would be offered to the first-name draw first, and T2 and T3, which force a last name, would then pass on `<base>`.)
  - It wraps `LibraryBasedPseudonymManager.load_library` to do that. `last_names=` or `places=` replace a list whole (T7, T8), before the `front` words are put at the head.
  - It replaces `library_manager.secrets` with a stand-in. `choice(seq)` returns `seq[0]` when `seq[0]` is a word of `front` not yet offered; otherwise it returns the real `secrets.choice(seq)`.
  - It wraps `DocumentProcessor._resolve_pseudonyms` to clear the "offered" set when it starts. So the first draw at write time is the forced word, whatever the command line's preview drew before.
  - `insert=False` (T21) moves a word to the head of a list only if the list already holds it; the default, `insert=True`, inserts it as before.
  - It counts the offers, and every test that forces a word checks `pre(offers > 0, ...)` (T21 after the fix forces nothing into the last names, so it checks the first-name offer only). T9, T11 and T14 force nothing and do not check it (T14 calls the custom check only): T9's stored row is reused without a draw, and `neutral_id` never draws from the lists. T7's `front` is `{"last": ["Quentrix"]}` and T8's `{"place": ["Zorbaville"]}`.
  - The library is patched in the process, so it reaches the Qt workers' threads. It does not reach the parallel batch's child processes: T1 calls `_process_single_document_worker` in the test's process.
- **Written:** read from the output file; for `.csv`, from the cell.
- Helpers are imported, underscore names only, from `tests/unit/test_custom_pseudonym_fixes_cli.py` (`_cli`, `_new_db`, `_at`, `pre`), `tests/unit/test_parallel_saved_pseudonym.py` (`_found`, `_detection`, `_settings`), and, for the desktop batch, `tests/unit/gui/test_custom_pseudonym_fixes_batch_worker.py` (`_Batch`).
