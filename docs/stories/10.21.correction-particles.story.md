## Acceptance Criteria

AC1 to AC12 are story-level (no epic text for 10.21). "AC precisions" adds detail.

1. **AC1 — A particle typed in small letters meets the same particle (the orchestrator's design).** In a correction, a word written entirely in small letters that is one of the particles « de », « du », « des », « le », « la », « les », « dit », « dite », « van », « von », « der », « den », « ter » meets only the same word written in small letters in the document, and only between two words of the same correction (never the last word it adds). It is never reached through a capitals form: « DU » typed never meets « du » (Lionel's capitals rule, 10.19's AC1). « d' » and « l' » are read inside their word, as 10.19 reads them (« d'Arvor »). Every other word is read as 10.19 reads it: the same word rule and the same bound between two words (`_words`, `_word_after`, `_between`).
2. **AC2 — The correction covers the whole name.** « Élorine » corrected as « Elorine du Prélis » or « ELORINE du Prélis » covers « Élorine du Prélis » whole, in the CLI ([E]) and in the desktop's single-document and batch reviews: no word of the name in clear.
3. **AC3 — Reached whole, or not at all, where the name goes on.** When the words the place holds start the correction, the correction adds words, a particle stands among them (typed, in any capitals, or written in small letters where the widening stops, an elided « d' » or « l' » included), the correction cannot be reached whole, and the name goes on at that place (AC precisions, AC3: a name word of the correction met there, or, past any particle written there, a word with the letters of the next name word typed), the place is never written with part of the name:
   - **CLI:** the place goes back to the review, as 10.19's places a joined group's correction does not reach do (`_apply_correction`): it is left unmodified and shown again right after the group, with the group's other such places.

The other rows of the name (batch review) take the edit as on `0be4073`, each checked at its own place the same way. In a single-document review, the row's other places keep today's rule.

### Fix Design (pre-decided)

1. **`validation/workflow.py`, beside `_between`:**
