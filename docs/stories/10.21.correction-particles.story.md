## Story

**Ordered by Lionel, 2026-10-26, on this leak class** (an added word left in clear after a correction): "fix it in 10.19" (`docs/stories/10.19.capitals-forms.story.md`, Decisions Recorded). 10.19's widening missed it for particles: a surname with « de », « du », « de la », « van der »... The release review calls it a P0 for 2.3.0; QA confirms it, realistic, and recommends blocking 2.3.0 on it. **Lionel, 2026-11-01:** "yes the du leak blocks 2.3.0"; and on the rule: "all the Élorine du Prélis, accented or not, all caps or not, should match" (Decisions Recorded, with every answer that settled the rule).

**As a** user who adds or corrects a name,
**I want** every writing of a name of two words or more to be found, whatever its accents and capitals, and a correction never applied to part of a name without being told,

### Fix Design (pre-decided)

1. **`validation/models.py`, `is_form(place, text)`** (Lionel's rule): after `place == text`, one condition: when `text` has two words or more (`len(text.split()) > 1`) and `fold(place).lower() == fold(text).lower()`, the place is a form. 2. **`validation/workflow.py`, beside `_between`:**
   - `_PARTICLES = frozenset({"de", "du", "des", "le", "la", "les", "dit", "dite", "van", "von", "der", "den", "ter"})`; `_ELIDED_PARTICLES = frozenset({"d'", "l'", "d’", "l’"})`.
   - `_holds_particle(word)`: `fold(word).lower()` in `_PARTICLES`, or its first two characters in `_ELIDED_PARTICLES` (any capitals). `_bare_particle(word)`: `fold(word).lower()` in `_PARTICLES` or in `_ELIDED_PARTICLES`.
   - `_extension(document_text, entity, new_text) -> tuple[tuple[int, int] | None, int | None]`: 10.19's `_widened_over_added_words` body. In the loop over the added words, a word is met when 10.19's bound holds (`_between`) and `fold(written).lower() == fold(word).lower()` (AC2; it replaces the capital test and `is_form` per word). Where the loop stops: no particle typed among the added words and none written there (`_holds_particle`): `(None, None)` (D1); a name word met before: `(None, end of the written word)`; else `(None, _continuation(...))`. After the loop, a last word met that is a particle: `(None, end of the next word)` when that word holds a capital, else `(None, None)`. A widened span returns `(span, None)`. `### Fixed`, a new bullet after 10.19's (AC7; the PM may prefer other wording):

- **One rule for a name's forms, and corrections with « de », « du », « van der »... (Story 10.21).** A name of two words or more is now found wherever its letters are written, whatever their accents and capitals, in small letters too: « Élorine du Prélis » reaches « ÉLORINE DU PRÉLIS », « Elorine du Prelis » and « élorine du prélis ». Two people whose names of two words differ only by an accent now share one pseudonym, and an ordinary two-word phrase written like a name is replaced with it. A name of one word is found as before: « Barre » never reaches « Barré », « Petit » never « petit ». So when you correct a name the review found only in part (« Élorine du Prélis » found as « Élorine »), the correction now covers the whole name, however you type it; it covered only the part found, leaving « du Prélis » in clear beside the pseudonym, silently, already in v2.2.0. In the command line that place comes back to the review, with no message; confirmed there as it is, it is written the same way. A correction without a particle changes in two cases: a particle the document writes and the correction leaves out (« Elorine Prélis » for « Élorine du Prélis »), and a given name written in capitals followed by an ordinary word in capitals (« ÉLORINE DE SON CÔTÉ »): such a place now comes back to the review, or stays to be reviewed, instead of being written.
