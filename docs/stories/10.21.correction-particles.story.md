## Acceptance Criteria

AC1 to AC12 are story-level (no epic text for 10.21). "AC precisions" adds detail.

1. **AC1 — A particle typed in small letters meets the same particle (the orchestrator's design).** In a correction, a word written entirely in small letters that is one of the particles « de », « du », « des », « le », « la », « les », « dit », « dite », « van », « von », « der », « den », « ter » meets only the same word written in small letters in the document, and only between two words of the same correction (never the last word it adds). It is never reached through a capitals form: « DU » typed never meets « du » (Lionel's capitals rule, 10.19's AC1). « d' » and « l' » are read inside their word, as 10.19 reads them (« d'Arvor »). Every other word is read as 10.19 reads it: the same word rule and the same bound between two words (`_words`, `_word_after`, `_between`).
2. **AC2 — The correction covers the whole name.** « Élorine » corrected as « Elorine du Prélis » or « ELORINE du Prélis » covers « Élorine du Prélis » whole, in the CLI ([E]) and in the desktop's single-document and batch reviews: no word of the name in clear.
3. **AC3 — Reached whole, or not at all, where the name goes on.** The rule applies when all of these hold:
   - the words the place holds start the correction, and the correction adds words;
   - a particle stands among them: typed, in any capitals; or written where the widening stops, in any capitals, an elided « d' » or « l' » included;
   - the correction cannot be reached whole;
   - the name goes on at that place (AC precisions, AC3).

   Then the place is never written with part of the name:
   - **CLI:** the place goes back to the review, as 10.19's places a joined group's correction does not reach do (`_apply_correction`): it is left unmodified and shown again right after the group, with the group's other such places.
   - **Desktop, single document:** the row takes the edit at every place it reaches whole. The places it would leave partly written are split off into one pending row of their own, which keeps the row's name and pseudonym preview; one undo puts them back with the edit. When the row's own place is one of them, the places reached move to the new row and take the edit there, and the row keeps its own place, pending.
   - **Desktop, batch:** a row whose place would be left partly does not take the edit and stays pending. The other rows of the name take it as on `0be4073`.
   - **Desktop, a row of another spelling of the name** (`_action_rows`, Lionel's 10.19 Q4) that 10.19's `_reaches` leaves out, for a correction holding a particle (the orchestrator's ruling on P5): a place of it the correction covers whole takes the edit (in a single document, split off from the row's other places); a place it would leave partly stays pending, and so does the rest of the row. Outside D1's scope, 10.19's `_reaches` alone.
   - **The message (both reviews):** whenever a place is left so, of the edited row, of another row the edit goes to, or of a row of another spelling, a message says why and names the first such place as it is written there (Q1).

   Where the name does not go on (the given name written alone, « Élorine de son côté », « Élorine la valide »), the place keeps today's span, as on `0be4073`. A pending place accepted as it is is written with its detected span, exactly as `0be4073` writes any pending row.
4. **AC4 — Another person's word is never taken.** The widening never covers a word that is not the typed word by the rules: « Mèzor » is not « Mézor » (10.19's rulings 1 and 2); a particle meets only the same particle.

### Fix Design (pre-decided)

1. **`validation/workflow.py`, beside `_between`:**
   - `_PARTICLES = frozenset({"de", "du", "des", "le", "la", "les", "dit", "dite", "van", "von", "der", "den", "ter"})`; `_ELIDED_PARTICLES = frozenset({"d'", "l'", "d’", "l’"})`.
   - `_holds_particle(word)`: `fold(word).lower()` in `_PARTICLES`, or its first two characters in `_ELIDED_PARTICLES` (any capitals). `_bare_particle(word)`: `fold(word).lower()` in `_PARTICLES` or in `_ELIDED_PARTICLES` (the particle and nothing else, any capitals).
   - `_extension(document_text, entity, new_text) -> tuple[tuple[int, int] | None, int | None]`: 10.19's `_widened_over_added_words` body, returning (the widened span or None, the end of the name written at the place where it goes on, or None). In the loop over the added words, every word, a particle included, first passes 10.19's bound check (`_between` the same in the document and in the correction, the PO's v1.1 fix); a typed word in `_PARTICLES` (small letters) whose written word is the same string is met; any other word passes 10.19's checks (a capital and `is_form` either way). Where the loop stops: when no particle is typed among the added words and the written word holds none (`_holds_particle`), `(None, None)` (D1: 10.19's rulings); when a name word of the correction was met before, `(None, end of the written word)`; else `(None, _continuation(...))`. After the loop, a last word met as a particle: `(None, end of the next word)` when that word holds a capital, else `(None, None)`. A widened span returns `(span, None)`.
   - `_continuation(document_text, new_text, typed, index, found, after_particle, bound) -> int | None`. `bound` is 10.19's bound check at the word where the loop stopped. It returns the end of the name, or None:
     - The written word where the loop stopped goes on itself when, at `bound`, it starts with an elided particle followed by a capital (its end).
     - Else it passes the bare particles written there (`_bare_particle`, any capitals) and the particles typed there (`_holds_particle`).
     - The next written word goes on when a particle was written or met (`after_particle`), at `bound`, and that word holds a capital (its end).
     - Else it goes on when it has the letters of the next typed name word and a particle stood between, written, typed or met just before (its end).
     - Else None.
   - `_widened_over_added_words` returns `_extension(...)[0]`; its docstring gains one sentence (AC1).
   - `ValidationWorkflow._left_partly(document_text, entity, new_text) -> int | None`: None when `_reached_span` finds the text or a form; else the second value of `_extension` when its span is None.
   - `ValidationWorkflow._holds_a_particle(new_text) -> bool`: a word of the correction (`_words`) holds a particle (`_holds_particle`): D1's scope, for P5's rows.
   - `_apply_correction`, the branch without joined parts: `elif self._left_partly(text, entity, new_text) is not None:` the place goes to `unreached[0]` and is not modified; else `_locate_modified_span` as today.
