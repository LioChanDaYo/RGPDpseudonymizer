## Acceptance Criteria

AC1 to AC12 are story-level (no epic text for 10.21). "AC precisions" adds detail.

1. **AC1 — A particle typed in small letters meets the same particle (the orchestrator's design).** In a correction, a word written entirely in small letters that is one of the particles « de », « du », « des », « le », « la », « les », « van », « von », « der », « den », « ter » meets only the same word written in small letters in the document, and only between two words of the same correction (never the last word it adds). It is never reached through a capitals form: « DU » typed never meets « du » (Lionel's capitals rule, 10.19's AC1). « d' » and « l' » are read inside their word, as 10.19 reads them (« d'Arvor »). Every other word is read as 10.19 reads it: the same word rule and the same bound between two words (`_words`, `_word_after`, `_between`).
2. **AC2 — The correction covers the whole name.** « Élorine » corrected as « Elorine du Prélis » or « ELORINE du Prélis » covers « Élorine du Prélis » whole, in the CLI ([E]) and in the desktop's single-document and batch reviews: no word of the name in clear.

### Fix Design (pre-decided)

1. **`validation/workflow.py`, beside `_between`:**
   - `_PARTICLES = frozenset({"de", "du", "des", "le", "la", "les", "van", "von", "der", "den", "ter"})`; `_ELIDED_PARTICLES = frozenset({"d'", "l'", "d’", "l’"})`.
   - `_holds_particle(word)`: `fold(word).lower()` in `_PARTICLES`, or its first two characters in `_ELIDED_PARTICLES` (any capitals). `_small_particle(word)`: `word` in `_PARTICLES`, or `word[:2]` in `_ELIDED_PARTICLES` (small letters only).
   - `_extension(document_text, entity, new_text) -> tuple[tuple[int, int] | None, bool]`: 10.19's `_widened_over_added_words` body, returning (span or None, particle). `particle` starts as whether any word the correction adds holds a particle (`_holds_particle`). In the loop over the added words, every word, a particle included, first passes 10.19's bound check (`_between` the same in the document and in the typed text; where it fails, the loop stops). Then a typed word in `_PARTICLES` (small letters) whose written word is the same string is met (remembered as `last_is_particle`). Any other word passes 10.19's word check (a capital, and `is_form` either way). Where the loop stops, it returns `(None, particle or _small_particle(written))`; after the loop, a last word met as a particle returns `(None, True)`.
   - `_widened_over_added_words` returns `_extension(...)[0]`; its docstring gains one sentence (AC1).
   - `ValidationWorkflow._left_partly(document_text, entity, new_text) -> bool`: False when `_reached_span` finds the text or a form; else `span is None and particle` from `_extension`.
   - `_apply_correction`, the branch without joined parts: `elif self._left_partly(text, entity, new_text):` the place goes to `unreached[0]` and is not modified; else `_locate_modified_span` as today.
2. **`gui/models/validation_state.py`:** `correction_left_partly(entity_id, new_text) -> bool`: any place of the row (`_current_places`) for which `ValidationWorkflow._left_partly` holds on the text it is in (`self._session.text_of(place)`), read at the span now written there. In `modify_entity_text`, the other rows kept are those of the same name or reached (`_reaches`) **and** not `correction_left_partly`.
